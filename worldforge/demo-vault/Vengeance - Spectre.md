# Vengeance — "Spectre"

Rezvani Vengeance build sheet, extracted from the official configurator (2026-09-22).

## Headline
- **Sum Total: $485,000** (base $285,000)
- The smart build: James Bond gadgets for less than the War Wagon's armor bill.

## What makes it the Spectre
- **Security Package** (+$42,000) — the full spook kit:
	- Smoke screen, military run-flat tires, reinforced suspension
	- Electromagnetic Pulse Protection, steel ram bumper
	- Electrified door handles, siren & horn options, strobe/blinding lights
	- Intercom system, magnetic dead bolts
	- Gas masks, first aid kit, hypothermia kit, pepper spray dispenser
- **Executive Seating** (+$125,000) — comfort where it counts
- Night vision system, center console safe, rear seat entertainment
- Solar power + roof-mounted solar charger/maintainer
- Operational front windows
