## Read the table
- Cheapest: **Dark Knight** ($425,800) — most horsepower per dollar, blackout stealth
- Most armored: **War Wagon** — B6 composite plus the B5 upgrade
- Most dramatic seats: **Tank** (Gladiatore, yellow stitching)
- Only one with escape gadgets: **Spectre** (smoke screen, EMP shield, ram bumper)
- Best off-grid: **Fortress** (Off-Grid Package)

Shared across the fleet: Shinedown logo (War Wagon & Spectre), roof LED light
bar, night vision, center console safe, solar options.
