The [[Veil Concordat]] maintains concealed infrastructure sites known internally as Hidden Access Facilities.

These locations function as secure entry points to protected operational spaces, data archives, and transit routes.

Rather than relying on visible fortification, Access Facilities are designed to remain psychologically invisible — integrated into mundane environments such as private libraries, decommissioned offices, or neglected institutional wings.

One documented system employs a rarely handled, dust-covered book as the initial activation trigger.

Removal of the book initiates a concealed sequencing mechanism requiring multiple additional volumes to be placed in a precise cumulative order.
Each volume represents only one fragment of the total access code.

Correct sequencing activates layered biometric verification systems.

Security protocols may include:

extended numeric passphrases

retinal identity confirmation

genetic authentication

mission-context authorization input

Successful validation reveals hidden structural pathways such as vertical descent shafts, shielded corridors, or subterranean transport nodes.

Access authorization is restricted to select Concordat personnel and cooperating operatives such as [[Lazriel]].
