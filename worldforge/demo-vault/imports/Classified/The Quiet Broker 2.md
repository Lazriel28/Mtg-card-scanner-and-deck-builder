The Quiet Broker functions as a high-level intermediary within the [[Veil Concordat]].

Their role involves delivering mission parameters, managing operational timing, and maintaining compartmentalization between strategic planners and field agents.

All communications are routed through indirect systems designed to prevent traceability.

Despite occupying an influential coordination position, the Broker’s identity remains unknown even to most Concordat members.

Primary operational contact: [[Lazriel]]
