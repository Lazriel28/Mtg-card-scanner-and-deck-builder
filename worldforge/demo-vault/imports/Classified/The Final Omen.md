“The Final Omen” is a fear-title used in underground intelligence circles to describe Lazriel’s confirmed operational presence.

The designation is not self-assigned.

It emerged from pattern analysis conducted by criminal networks and later adopted by several intelligence agencies.

When this title appears in intercepted communication, it indicates that a situation has progressed beyond negotiation or containment.

The term reflects the belief that Lazriel’s involvement marks the irreversible closing stage of an unfolding crisis.
