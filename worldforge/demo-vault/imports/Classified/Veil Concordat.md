The Veil Concordat is a covert equilibrium organization dedicated to preventing destabilizing global events.

Its influence operates through deniable intervention, psychological containment strategies, and symbolic enforcement.

Operational doctrine prioritizes outcomes over legality.

Field operatives are compartmentalized and rarely aware of the broader strategic intent behind assignments.

The Concordat maintains [[Hidden Access Facilities]], coded mythologies, and legacy access systems to protect institutional continuity as well as a infornmant known as [[The Quiet Broker]].
