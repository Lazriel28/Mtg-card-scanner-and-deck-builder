The Legend Statement is a phrase recorded in multiple intelligence archives and underground narratives.

It is believed to originate from an unidentified corrective operative active during periods of geopolitical instability.

The statement reads:

"I am the crow that soars in silence.  
The dagger that falls unseen.  
Where shadow meets purpose, I am the line drawn in blood."

The phrase is interpreted as both warning and philosophical declaration.

It has influenced modern symbolic intimidation practices and myth-construction strategies within covert organizations.
