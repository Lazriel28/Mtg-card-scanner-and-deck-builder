At every completed contract, Lazriel leaves a pristine white card.

This mark is known as the [[Crow Sigil]].

Symbol Description

A raven diving downward

A dagger forming the spine

Wings curved like a mourning veil

A skull hidden in negative space

Matte black ink with faint crimson accents

The card represents:

inevitability

silence

final judgment

Victims rarely understand its meaning.
Survivors build their lives around it.
