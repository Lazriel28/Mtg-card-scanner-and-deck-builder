Lazriel functions as a living corrective force within the world.

They do not serve chaos.
They do not serve order.

They serve [[Balance]].

Where systems destabilize — politically, socially, existentially — Lazriel appears.
Not as a conqueror, but as a scalpel.

Their actions often prevent greater catastrophes…
though the moral cost remains debated.

Many scholars classify Lazriel as an embodiment of necessary consequence.
